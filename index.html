Sources you can open

Grocery prices · BLS Consumer Price Index

Annual means of the monthly, not-seasonally-adjusted CPI-U “Food at home” series. National series: CUUR0000SAF11. Regional series: Northeast, Midwest, South, West. CPI is a price index, not dollars spent.

Income · BEA National Income and Product Accounts

Personal income per capita, annual dollars, not seasonally adjusted. “Personal income” includes multiple income sources; it is not household wages, take-home pay, or median income. Both national lines use nominal values and are separately indexed to 1970 = 100.

Food budget share · BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey

Table 1800, Census regions and Table 2700, Census divisions. Values are average annual dollars over 2021–2022; the map computes food-at-home mean ÷ income-before-taxes mean. BLS geographic CEX availability varies by geography and period.

Geography · U.S. Census Bureau definitions

Census regions and divisions. State outlines come from the us-atlas TopoJSON project, based on U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundaries. Outlines help orient; the statistics belong to the grouped Census geography.

How to read the two measures
  • Indexed growth: annual-average food-at-home CPI-U ÷ 1970 annual-average CPI-U × 100; BEA annual personal income per capita ÷ its own 1970 value × 100. Every year from 1970 to 2024 has a full annual value. Data after 2024 are excluded because the packaged 2025 CPI series has a missing month. Regional food CPI indexes use each region’s 1978 full-year mean as 100. The division view uses the containing region’s series because BLS does not publish a comparable CPI food-at-home series for Census divisions in this dataset.
  • Food budget share: BLS mean annual food-at-home expenditure ÷ BLS mean annual income before taxes × 100. The source estimate is a two-calendar-year mean (2021–2022); it must not be read as a single year. The ratio of means does not equal the mean of individual consumer-unit ratios.
  • Not a full affordability measure: CPI and personal income per capita describe broad trends, not a grocery basket for a particular person. Neither line accounts for taxes, benefits, household size, housing costs, income distribution, or changes in shopping behavior.
  • Map: colors group state outlines into the selected Census division/region. Select a division from the map or the synchronized list. A state does not have its own observation in this graphic.
  • Data vintage: annual CPI and income series are packaged through 2024. BLS regional/division spending rows are the observed 2021–2022 geographic mean tables cited above. Snapshot dates differ by source.
